# ML recovery: constant rates through episodic ClaSSE

Current publication benchmark: [runs/publication-100](runs/publication-100/README.md).

Three scenarios (Slow, Medium, Fast), five extant-tip groups, 100 independent trees per group: 1,500 trees and 4,500 starts. Two epochs with all rates linked; known boundary at half prescribed stem duration. Three free rates. No separate GeoSSE fits in this publication run.

Use [run status](runs/publication-100/run-status.md) for retrieved completeness and [submission record](runs/publication-100/submission-status.md) for job IDs. Final recovery outputs appear only after all expected fits are available.

Historical 50-replicate results are imported separately in runs/pilot-50-evidence and must not be mixed with publication replicates.
